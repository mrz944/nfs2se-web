      How to Race Online

      1. Get the game into this browser. Wait for the game files to download automatically. This happens once; next time they are already cached here.
      2. Pick a name. Type it under Online Play → Your name. It's what the others see in the room.
      3. Make a room. One player clicks Create Room, gives it a name, and optionally a password and a player limit (2–8). Untick Show in the room list for a private room.
      4. Bring your friends in. Click Copy invite link and send it, or let them pick the room in the Rooms list and click Join. Everyone in the room is listed with their ping and direct or via server.
      5. Everyone clicks Start Game. Wait until all players see the game's menu.
      6. Switch the game to network. In the game's main menu: Game Setup → game type: network → DONE. RACE becomes CONNECT: click it, type your name, keep protocol IPX.
      7. Host the race. The player shown as Best host picks create game and lands in the race lobby.
      8. Join the race. The others pick join game and choose the host's game. Everyone appears in the lobby with their car.
      9. Get ready. Pick your car and settings, then press RACE (your light turns green). When everyone is ready, the host's RACE starts the race.
      10. Race. The host picks the track in the lobby. The badge at the top left shows the room and each racer's ping. Click it to copy the invite link.
      Lag
      Smoother play: your own car reacts in your game straight away, whoever hosts, and the other cars appear about half a ping late. The lowest-ping player still makes the best host.
      Via server
      If two browsers can't connect directly (strict routers), packets go through this server instead. It works, just a little slower.
      Keep it visible
      Leave every game window open while racing. A minimised tab can fall behind.
      Names
      Your name here is filled in for you in the game when you click CONNECT (up to 8 characters).
      Controllers
      Gamepads and wheels work: turn them on in Game Settings, press a button on yours before Start Game (the browser only shows it after a press), then set it up in Options → Controllers. On a pad the triggers are gas and brake and Start pauses.

      Original Game · Our Work

      Untouched: the real NFS II SE

      • Handling and physics: every car drives exactly like on a 1997 PC. The original code runs, recompiled, not rewritten or re-tuned.
      • Cars, tracks and AI: the original game data and opponent logic, the Special Edition cars and tracks included.
      • Music and sound: the original soundtrack and effects through the game's own sound engine.
      • Menus and modes: Single Race, Tournament, Knockout, Showcase, as you remember them.

      Added: what makes it run here

      • Static recompilation: the game's x86 code is translated to C and WebAssembly, so it runs natively in the browser with no PC emulator.
      • Voodoo on your GPU: the 3dfx Glide 2 graphics path is redrawn with WebGL 2, keeping the Voodoo fog, depth and blending, and scaled up sharply.
      • Internet multiplayer: the game's LAN mode now works over the internet. Rooms with passwords and a room browser, direct browser-to-browser links with relay fallback, and a best-host pick so the lag goes to the fewest players.
      • Browser comfort: one-time download into browser storage, mouse capture, audio, smart pacing when the tab is hidden.

      The game runs from the original 3dfx executable of the Special Edition CD (NFS2SEA.EXE). NFSIISE by zaps166 was a helpful reference.
